The sea starts at your step

Three resorts on one stretch of Red Sea coast, with a house reef close enough to reach before breakfast.

One bay. Three resorts.

Marsa Alam has the clearest water on the Egyptian coast and almost none of the crowd that finds it. Our three resorts sit on the same stretch of it — walking distance apart, sharing one reef, one dive centre, and one reservations desk.

Which means you book once, and move between them freely: dinner at one, a dive from another, the quiet pool at the third.
